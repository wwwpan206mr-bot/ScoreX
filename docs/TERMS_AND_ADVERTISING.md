# ScoreX Terms of Use and Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Support: inf.scorex@gmail.com

## Terms of use
ScoreX provides cricket information for general informational purposes. Public score browsing does not require an account. Match data, timings, statistics and commentary may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Do not rely on ScoreX for wagering or financial decisions. Users must not misuse the service or attempt unauthorized access. Third-party content remains subject to its owners' rights and terms.

## Advertising principles
ScoreX supports clearly labeled Google AdMob banner and native advertisements. Advertising must not obscure scores, interfere with navigation, imitate app controls or encourage accidental clicks. Sponsored content must be distinguishable from editorial match information. Advertising must comply with the applicable advertising network policies, platform rules and local laws.

## Privacy and consent
Google Mobile Ads processes advertising-related data as described in the Privacy Policy. Google UMP checks consent before requests and exposes privacy choices where required. Test ads are enabled until publisher configuration is supplied.

## Contact
Questions, complaints and privacy requests: inf.scorex@gmail.com.

## Changes
We may update these terms as features and third-party services change. The revision date will be updated.

ScoreX includes labelled banner and native AdMob placements in the match centre. Test ads are enabled by default and do not generate earnings.
